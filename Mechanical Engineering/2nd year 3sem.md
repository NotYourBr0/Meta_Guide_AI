### Practical / Lab Syllabus

1. **(a)** Study of various crystal structures through models:
   - BCC (Body Centred Cubic)
   - FCC (Face Centred Cubic)
   - HCP (Hexagonal Close Packed)
   - Tetrahedral voids
   - Octahedral voids
   **(b)** Material identification of approximately 50 common items kept in a box

2. Specimen preparation for metallographic examination / microstructural examination:
   - Cutting
   - Grinding
   - Polishing
   - Etching

3. Comparative study of microstructures of different given specimens:
   - Mild steel
   - Gray Cast Iron
   - Brass
   - Copper
   - Other given specimens

4. Heat treatment experiments and comparison of hardness before and after:
   - Annealing
   - Normalizing
   - Quenching
   - Case hardening

5. Study of microstructure and hardness of steel at different rates of cooling
   Microstructure examination of white cast iron

6. Perform mechanical tests on a given material to determine its various mechanical properties under:
   - Tensile loading
   - Compressive loading
   - Shear loading
   - Torsional loading

7. To determine hardness of a given material using:
   - Rockwell hardness test
   - Vickers hardness test
   - Brinell hardness test

8. To perform Impact test on a given material and to determine its resilience

9. To study and perform Fatigue test on a given material and to determine its fatigue strength

10. To perform Bending test and to determine the Young's Modulus of Elasticity via deflection of beam

11. Creep testing on creep testing machine
