## COURSE INFORMATION
- **Course Code:** 3CE4-24
- **Course Name:** Civil Engineering Materials Lab
- **Branch:** Civil Engineering
- **Year/Semester:** II Year / III Semester
- **Credits:** 1
- **L-T-P Structure:** 0L + 0T + 2P
- **Maximum Marks:** 100
  - Internal Assessment (IA): 60
  - End Term Exam (ETE): 40

---

## LIST OF EXPERIMENTS

**Experiment 1: To determine properties of following materials:**

**A. Stone:**
- a. Compressive strength
- b. Water absorption
- c. Impact value
- d. Tensile strength

**B. Bricks:**
- a. Water absorption
- b. Compressive strength
- c. Dimension and Tolerance

**C. Tiles:**
- a. Water absorption
- b. Tolerance
- c. Impact value

**D. Timber:**
- Compressive and Tensile Strength of Timber across and along the Grain

**Experiment 2:** To Study the Properties and Utilization of Fly Ash in Construction

**Experiment 3:** To Study the Different Aluminum and Steel Sections

**Experiment 4:** To Study the Manufacturing and Use of Concrete Hollow Blocks

**Experiment 5:** To Study the Properties and Uses of Kota Stone and its Slurry
